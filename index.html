Malbec
StyleFull body, medium-high tannin. Originated in France, made famous by Argentina.
AromasDark plum, blackberry, violet, cocoa, tobacco, leather. High altitude = more floral lift.
Key regionsMendoza (AR) — Luján de Cuyo & Uco Valley. Cahors (FR) — leaner, more tannic.

Port
StyleSweet, fortified red. Brandy added during fermentation to stop it — retains sugar. 19–22% ABV.
TypesRuby — young, fruity. Tawny — oak-aged, nutty, caramel. Vintage — declared years, age-worthy. LBV — Late Bottled Vintage.
RegionDouro Valley (PT). Key grapes: Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca, Tinta Roriz.
ServeRuby: 14–16°C · Tawny: 10–12°C · Port glass or small tulip
Pairs with
StiltonDark chocolateWalnutsChristmas cake
Sherry
StyleBone dry to very sweet. Made via solera system (blending across vintages). Underrated.
TypesFino/Manzanilla — bone dry, pale, saline. Amontillado — nutty, amber. Oloroso — rich, dark, dry. PX — intensely sweet, raisin syrup.
RegionJerez de la Frontera (ES). Palomino grape for dry; PX grape for sweet.
ServeFino: 7–9°C · Oloroso: 12–14°C · Copita glass
Pairs with
Tapas (Fino)Nuts (Amontillado)Ice cream (PX)Jamón
Madeira
StyleFortified, heated during production. Almost indestructible once opened. Very age-worthy.
TypesSercial — dry, high acidity. Verdelho — off-dry, smoky. Bual — medium sweet. Malmsey — rich & sweet.
RegionMadeira Island (PT). Volcanic terroir, high acidity balances sweetness.
ServeDry: 10–12°C · Sweet: 12–14°C · White wine or dessert glass
Pairs with
Soup (Sercial)NutsCaramel dessertsBlue cheese

RegionsDouro (PT), Jerez (ES), Madeira Island (PT)

FranceRegion
BordeauxCab Sauv & Merlot blends. Left Bank = Cab Sauv dominant. Right Bank = Merlot dominant.
BurgundyPinot Noir (red), Chardonnay (white). Terroir-driven. Côte de Nuits vs Côte de Beaune.
ChampagneChardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier. Traditional method. Only "Champagne" if from this region.
RhôneN. Rhône: Syrah (red), Viognier (white). S. Rhône: Grenache blends, Châteauneuf-du-Pape.
LoireSauvignon Blanc (Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé), Chenin Blanc, Cab Franc (Chinon), Muscadet.
AlsaceRiesling, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Gris. Aromatic, dry to sweet. Labelled by grape, not region.
ItalyRegion
TuscanySangiovese. Chianti, Brunello di Montalcino, Vino Nobile. "Super Tuscans" = Cab/Merlot blends.
PiedmontNebbiolo = Barolo & Barbaresco. High tannin, high acidity, age-worthy. Also Barbera, Dolcetto.
VenetoProsecco (sparkling), Pinot Grigio, Amarone (dried grape, rich red), Soave (white).
SicilyNero d'Avola (red), Marsala (fortified). Warm climate, ripe fruit styles.
SpainRegion
RiojaTempranillo-based. Oak aging: Crianza → Reserva → Gran Reserva (increasing age).
Ribera del D.Tempranillo (Tinto Fino). Bold, structured reds. High altitude = good acidity.
CavaTraditional method sparkling. Macabeo, Xarello, Parellada grapes. Dry, citrus, almond.
JerezSherry. Fino (dry, pale) → Amontillado → Oloroso → Pedro Ximénez (sweet, dark).
New WorldRegion
AustraliaBarossa: big Shiraz. Coonawarra: Cab Sauv on terra rossa. Margaret River: Chardonnay, Cab Sauv. Clare/Eden: Riesling.
New ZealandMarlborough: benchmark Sauvignon Blanc. Central Otago: Pinot Noir. Hawke's Bay: Cab blends.
USANapa: Cab Sauv, full-bodied. Sonoma: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir. Oregon: Willamette Valley Pinot Noir.
ArgentinaMendoza: flagship Malbec — rich, dark fruit, velvety tannins. High altitude (800–1500m) = freshness. Also Bonarda, Torrontés (white).
ChileMaipo: Cab Sauv. Colchagua: Merlot, Carménère (Chile's signature). Casablanca/San Antonio: cool-climate Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir.
South AfricaStellenbosch: Cab Sauv, Chenin Blanc. Swartland: Syrah, Chenin. Pinotage = SA signature grape.
Canada (BC)Okanagan: Pinot Gris, Riesling, Merlot, Cab Franc. Short season = aromatic whites & elegant reds.
